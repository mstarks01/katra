## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** On both full runs the lanes lose must-finds in their own drafts. On the sweep, 8 drafts state another mechanism at the reference's place, and the maintainer ruled each `different`. On the run, 13 drafts write another verb and 6 state another attacker position. 4 of the 6 Phase 2 targets are in the run's cases, and all 4 still miss with a stated position. No run shows a loss after generation.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives remain?** Analysis, for the generation rows, as an association. On the sweep, the leads reached the lane on all 8 substitutions, and the signed place facts arrived where a fact is signed. For the `unled` rows: 06/15 and 09/18 lack a rule over facts the model holds (preparation); 08/19 is an extraction gap, by the maintainer's decision of 2026-11-19; 06/14 and 07/18 have no traced cause. The 66 sweep rows with no attribution have no phase.

**What is the proposed fix?** None in this audit. The QA-2026-11-17-13 design is the Phase 2 intervention for the 6 targets.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** No code changed. The audit traced both runs (`-E1`, `-E2`), and re-ran the scorecard and the delivery trace at `2235a71c` because the rows it cites are stale.

**What did not work?** `-E1` is refuted: the sweep cannot attribute 66 of its 100 rows.

**What remains untested or blocked?**
- The 19 must-finds of cases 14, 15 and 17 to 20, which neither run holds.
- A position for every lane on cases 02 to 05, 07 and 16.
- The meaning of the 68 structural matches (21 sweep, 47 run) and of the run's 19 unruled generation rows.
- 06/14 and 07/18 until #2054 group 2 gives authority facts.
- Run-to-run variation: each run is one run.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0 of $0.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?** One question: does the QA-2026-11-17-13 design recover the Phase 2 targets on the sweep's model? The answer decides whether the design is kept. On the run, 4 of the 6 targets still miss with a stated position, so the design has targets to recover; the run is on another model and does not answer for Sonnet. This audit proposes no spend. The audit that asks for it writes the eight answers of `references/experiment-protocol.md` first. Its smallest stage is likely a lane replay of the tampering and information-disclosure lanes on the target cases, as QA-2026-10-27-01-E2 and QA-2026-11-03-01 used, not a full run. Its paired passes must hold the design's controls, and only 6 of the 21 sweep controls are in the 6 target cases.

**What should the next audit do first?** Price the design's lane-replay test against its falsifier and controls, and ask the maintainer for the amount. After any run, run `trace.py` on it and send the structural matches and the near misses as one ballot.

## Semantic review

These instructions are for the ballot after a run on today's contract.

- **What a row asks.** The reference, its ruled attacker position, and the draft at its place with the position the draft states. The question: does the draft state the reference's attack, with the same attacker, target and access path?
- **The answers.** `same`, `different` or `unclear`. `unclear` keeps the row open; it never counts as a match.
- **Which rows.** Every structural match, because a match by verb, place and position can still name another mechanism (QA-2026-11-02-05-E8). Every near miss that `run.py near-misses` writes.
- **What the reader does not see.** The arm or the run, where a ballot compares arms.
- **Where the answers go.** `run.py near-miss-rulings` for the near misses, and the ledger row of the audit for the matches.

## Reproduction

Every command runs offline, from a checkout at `2235a71c`. The two runs are not in git, so the commands run only on the maintainer's machine.

```bash
D=evals/experiments/QA-2026-11-24-01
RUN=~/.local/share/katra/paid-runs/2026-11-19-stride-8case-42b63219
KATRA_OFFLINE=1 PYTHONPATH=src:. uv run python $D/trace.py \
  evals/runs/20261024T-sonnet-medium-sweep evals/corpus \
  evals/experiments/QA-2026-11-17-13/verb-rows.csv $D/sweep-2026-10-24
KATRA_OFFLINE=1 PYTHONPATH=src:. uv run python $D/trace.py $RUN evals/corpus - $D/run-2026-11-19
python3 $D/manifest.py evals/runs/20261024T-sonnet-medium-sweep $D/sweep-2026-10-24/manifest.json
python3 $D/manifest.py $RUN $D/run-2026-11-19/manifest.json
```

`manifest.py` writes the run's directory name, so each command rebuilds its `manifest.json` byte for byte. `trace.py` reads each artifact's mode, and raises on a mode, cause or verb ruling it has no rule for.
