## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** Zone recall falls after #2381, from 0.507 to 0.464 over 5 sweeps a side. In case 14, the one case read, the model spells the partner zone ID differently and leaves about as many elements with no zone (9 in A, 10 in B over 5 sweeps). Cases 05 and 17 give most of the rest of the fall, and nobody read them. The effect on findings is not measured.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives remain?** Extraction. The confidence is low for the cause. Two alternatives remain. First, the #2381 prompt text changes how the model names a boundary. Second, the spelling varies from sweep to sweep, and 5 sweeps a side do not separate it from chance: arm C, which carries #2381, writes the reference ID in 2 of 5 sweeps.

**What is the proposed fix?** None. A fix needs a known cause.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** `-E1` measures #2381 as merged. `-E2` measures the #2383 candidate on branch `extraction-flow-id-by-code`.

**What did not work?** `-E3` is null: the #2307 draft shows no harm, and it shows no gain because no input can show one. `-E2` is refuted. When code writes each flow ID and the prompt asks for none, an assumption can still name a flow by an ID. That ID does not resolve, and case 03 fails the gate in 3 of 5 sweeps.

**What remains untested or blocked?** The cause of the zone fall. The effect of #2381 on findings: the sweeps ran in extraction mode, and no `run.py gain` read was made. The effect on ASVS applicability.
