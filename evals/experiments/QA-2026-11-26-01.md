## The eight answers

**1. The question, and the decision.** On the sweep's model (Sonnet 5, medium thinking), does a lane that is given every model-supported mechanism at each led place write the holder's attack at 2 or more of the 6 target places more than `main` does? It must also keep every control, add no more than 50% proposals, and raise the unsupported share by no more than 5 percentage points. A supported stage 1 leads to stage 2. A supported stage 2 merges the treatment. Any other result keeps `main`, records the row, and closes Phase 2 of #2204 for this mechanism.

**2. Offline checks done.** `-E1` and `-E2` above. On `0df64827`: the 39 tests of `tests/test_stride_mechanisms.py` and the full suite (20,806 passed). The list reaches the lane block of 5 targets through today's `prepare`.

**3. Ledger and archive search.** `experiments --signature "a lane drafts at the reference's place under another action, and the draft is not the reference's attack"` returns QA-2026-11-17-13-E2 and -E3 (refuted, about a verb fix) and QA-2026-11-24-01-E2 (supported: the targets still miss on terra). The design's prior refutations are other interventions: an ordering paragraph on terra (QA-2026-10-27-01-E2), a grant-holder exemplar (QA-2026-11-03-01-E3) and a completeness sentence (QA-2026-09-26-01-E2, -E3). No row and no archive holds a lane that was given a mechanism list. No compatible result exists.

**4. Why no free check answers it.** The question is how a model reads a new input. No archived run holds the list, and a scripted answer proves nothing about a model.

**5. Stage and cases.** `lane-replay --prepare-today signed`, one lane call for each case and lane, before the fan-in and the critic. The `signed` arm gives each case's signed facts as the catalog. The sweep's catalog came from its own assertion pass, so the two arms are compared with each other and not with the sweep. No holdout case is read.
- Stage 1, 8 lane runs: the lane of each of the 5 reachable targets (03/8, 06/3, 09/4, 10/17, 13/19); the tampering lane of case 02, which holds the present draft of 02/3; and the tampering lane of case 03 and the information-disclosure lane of case 09, for controls 03/3 and 09/13. Controls 06/4 and 10/4 sit in target lanes.
- Stage 2, 8 lane runs, only after a supported stage 1: the other 8 controls in a tampering or information-disclosure lane (04/3, 04/8, 07/6, 07/12, 08/4, 08/10, 11/4, 12/3).
- No run for the other 9 controls and the 6 "same" rows: they sit in the spoofing, repudiation, denial-of-service and elevation-of-privilege lanes, whose requests `-E1` shows are byte-identical.

**6. Metric and plan.**
- Arms: control from a tree at the `main` commit under the rebased treatment, and treatment from the rebased branch (see Baseline comparability), with one tier file and `KATRA_SAMPLING_STRONG_THINKING=medium`. Three passes of each arm. The arms alternate inside each pass.
- Primary metric: targets recovered, out of the design's 6. A target is recovered in an arm when a draft at the target place states the holder's attack in 2 or more of its 3 passes. The maintainer rules each draft at a target place by meaning, blind to the arm. Target 02/3 cannot be recovered, so the design's bar stays as written: the treatment must recover at least 2 more of the 6 targets than the control.
- Controls: the 12 matched controls in these two lanes, and the present on-path or storage-copy draft of each of the 6 targets. A control is lost when a treatment pass does not hold it. As the design writes it, the treatment fails when it loses any control in more than 1 of its 3 passes, whatever the control arm does. The record also reports how often the control arm holds each control, so that a reader can see a control that neither arm holds on the `signed` catalog.
- Burden: proposals for each lane, summed over the passes; the treatment fails above 1.5 times the control.
- Unsupported share: the maintainer labels each draft of both arms that matches no reference, blind to the arm. The treatment fails when its unsupported share is more than 5 percentage points above the control's. At the sweep's rates (about 6 tampering and 9 information-disclosure drafts a call), stage 1 holds about 165 control and up to 245 treatment drafts.
- Each lane run writes its record and its whole proposals with `--out`; the blind ballot reads those files.
- Pre-flight: the first lane run of pass 1, both arms. Read its record (commit, clean tree, case, lane, catalog arm, corpus digest) and the reported charge before the rest. The record holds no model and no thinking setting, so the calling loop writes the tier file and the `KATRA_SAMPLING_*` settings it passes beside each record.
- Stop for futility after pass 2: the maintainer rules the drafts at the target places of passes 1 and 2, blind to the arm. If fewer than 2 targets have a treatment draft that states the holder's attack, no result can reach the bar, so the run stops. A position label does not decide this: targets 02/3 and 09/4 have no ruled position, and the primary metric reads meaning.

**7. Spend.** Priced from the sweep's own tokens at the harness's Sonnet 5 rates ($2 input, $10 output per million tokens), with the treatment's output at 1.5 times the sweep's (`price.txt`):
- Stage 1: $3.60 a pass of both arms, $10.80 for three. Cap: $12.50, which leaves room for 5 retried calls at up to $0.31 each.
- Stage 2: $3.73 a pass, $11.20 for three. Cap: $13.00, which leaves room for 4 retried calls at up to $0.38 each. It is asked for only after stage 1.
- `lane-replay` holds no spend: it reads no amount from `--accept-cost`, and it records no token usage. The calling loop holds the cap. After each call it adds the charge the provider reported, and it stops before a call that could pass the cap at the dearest call's price.
- Route: only a vendor that reports a charge lets the loop hold the cap. The `anthropic` vendor, which the sweep ran on, reports none (`reports_charge` in `src/katra/vendors.py`). So the run goes through `openrouter` to `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5`, the same model on another vendor. This is a change from the sweep, and it needs the maintainer's yes. Through `anthropic` the loop could only count calls at the estimated price.

**8. A null, adverse or inconclusive result.**
- Null (fewer than 2 more targets): the row reads `refuted`; `main` stays; the branch stays unmerged as the record of the treatment; Phase 2 of #2204 closes for this mechanism, and `needs-sweep` comes off.
- Adverse (a control lost, the burden cap passed, or the unsupported rule broken): the same, and the row names the control, the lane and the draft.
- Inconclusive (a lane run fails after its retries, a pass does not finish, or the ballot does not come back): no conclusion. Only the missing lane runs are asked for again, with a new amount.

## Limits

- The price is an estimate from one sweep of each case. Thinking tokens vary from call to call.
- The `signed` arm is not the sweep's catalog. A target that the lane recovers with signed facts can still miss behind a weaker extraction.
- One model. A result says nothing about terra or another vendor's model.
- A lane replay stops before the fan-in and the critic. The 9 controls and 6 "same" rows in other lanes keep byte-identical requests, but in a full run more tampering and information-disclosure drafts could still change what the fan-in or the critic keeps. No stage reads that.
- Lane output varies from 7,569 to 20,501 tokens across the priced cases of the sweep, and thinking varies from call to call. The cap, not the estimate, bounds the spend.
- The design's second signature, 12/17 and 13/16, is read and not targeted. 12/17 sits in the case 12 tampering lane of stage 2, so stage 2 reads it. 13/16 sits in the elevation-of-privilege lane, whose request does not change.

## Extension tickets raised

Proposed, not filed (the capability extension mode is "propose only"):

- **`lane-replay` holds no spend and records no usage.** `--accept-cost` is required, but the command reads no amount from it and calls no `consent.gate` or `consent.hold`, unlike `run` and `judge-replay`. It passes no token record to `node_call`, so its record states no tokens, and on a vendor that reports no charge it states no cost. Its record also names no model and no sampling. A replay on such a vendor cannot hold a cap or state its `actual_usd`.
