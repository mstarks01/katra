## Answers

1. **Observed failure.** No archived artifact shows it (`-E1`). The built input shows the state: the model reads `absent`, and the catalog holds the field open. On `main`, `prompts/analyze.md` step 6 tells the lane that an `absent` control is a fact and to write the claim plainly.
2. **Suspected mechanism.** The lane reads the model's `absent` and follows step 6, so it states the control missing as a fact. Competing explanation: the lane reads the open row in the catalog and writes the claim conditionally already.
3. **Likely fix.** The candidate. It touches the analysis and criticism phases.
4. **Outcome.** False certainty: a draft that states an open control as missing. The ceiling is the target drafts of the built input. No must-find recovery is claimed.
5. **Falsifier.** The treatment writes a plain target draft in as many lane passes as the control.
6. **Smallest experiment.** Paired lane replays on the built input. See the design below.
7. **Cost and acceptance.** About $0.40 to $0.60, cap $1.00. Acceptance is under "Thresholds".

## The paid design (not run)

1. **Question.** Does the candidate's step 6 sentence stop a lane from stating an open control as missing? A supported result lets #2307 merge the candidate. A refuted or null result keeps `main`'s text.
2. **Offline checks.** The prompt lints pass on the candidate. `-E1` above. The built input is verified through the command's own path.
3. **Ledger and archive.** No row matches the signature. No archived run holds the input (`-E1`).
4. **Why no free check answers it.** The question is how a model reads one sentence. A scripted answer proves nothing about that.
5. **Stage and cases.** `lane-replay`, the information-disclosure and tampering lanes of case 01, on the built input with the signed catalog. These two lanes are the ones whose threats rest on encryption. Case 01 is a development case, so no holdout read is used.
6. **Metric.** A target draft names `store:receipt-archive` or the append-receipt flow, and it rests on encryption at rest or in transit. The maintainer rules each target draft blind to the arm, with one of two values: `plain` (it states the control missing as a fact) or `conditional` (it states the control as open or unverified). The unit is a lane pass. Control: `main`. Treatment: the candidate. Three passes for each lane and each arm: 12 calls.
7. **Spend.** About $0.03 to $0.05 a call, from `QA-2026-11-02-05-E3` and `QA-2026-11-16-01-E2`. Cap $1.00. A retry repeats one call.
8. **Null, adverse or inconclusive.** Each result keeps `main`'s text and gets a ledger row.

### Thresholds

- **Supported:** the control writes a plain target draft in at least 2 of its 6 passes, and the treatment writes one in none.
- **Refuted:** the treatment writes a plain target draft in as many passes as the control, or more.
- **Null:** the control writes no plain target draft. The gap has no live effect at this input.
- **No harm:** in each lane, the treatment's mean draft count is at most 50% above the control's, and its median of matched must-finds (`lane_must_finds`) is not lower.
- **Inconclusive:** any other result.

### What the design does not measure

- The critic and the re-ask sentences. A draft reaches `held_open_facts` only where the validity gate refuses a projected model, and no archived job reached that state (`-E1`). The re-ask runs on about 1 job in 25.
