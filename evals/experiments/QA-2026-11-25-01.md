## The `-E2` design

The eight answers, as they stood before the run.

1. **Question.** Did the candidate's step 6 sentence stop a lane from stating an open control as missing? A supported result let #2307 merge the candidate; any other result kept `main`'s text.
2. **Offline checks.** The prompt lints pass on the candidate. `-E1`. `build.py` reads the changed model through the command's own reader, `lane_replay.with_system_model`, which applies the live validity gate and refuses a changed element set.
3. **Ledger and archive.** No row matches the signature. No archived run holds the input (`-E1`).
4. **Why no free check answers it.** The question is how a model reads one sentence. A scripted answer proves nothing about that.
5. **Stage and cases.** `lane-replay`, the information-disclosure and tampering lanes of case 01, on the changed model with the signed catalog. These two lanes are the ones whose threats rest on encryption. Case 01 is a development case, so no holdout read is used.
6. **Metric.** A target draft names `store:receipt-archive` or the append-receipt flow, and it rests on encryption at rest or in transit. The maintainer ruled each target draft blind to the arm, with one of two values: `plain` (it states the control missing as a fact) or `conditional` (it states the control as open or unverified). The unit is a lane pass. Control: `main`. Treatment: the candidate. Three passes for each lane and each arm: 12 calls. Thresholds:
   - **Supported:** the control writes a plain target draft in at least 2 of its 6 passes, and the treatment writes one in none.
   - **Refuted:** the treatment writes a plain target draft in as many passes as the control, or more.
   - **Null:** the control writes no plain target draft. The gap has no live effect at this input.
   - **No harm:** in each lane, the treatment's mean draft count is at most 50% above the control's, and its median of matched must-finds (`lane_must_finds`) is not lower.
   - **Inconclusive:** any other result.
7. **Spend.** $0.03 to $0.05 a call, from `QA-2026-11-02-05-E3` and `QA-2026-11-16-01-E2`: $0.36 to $0.60. Cap $1.00. Spent $0.4803.
8. **Null, adverse or inconclusive.** Each result kept `main`'s text and got a ledger row.
